Privacy policy

Fiel d.o.o., Rušiščak 5b, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia ("Fiel") respects your privacy and processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Croatian data protection law.

What data we collect

We only collect the data you send us yourself by e-mail, phone or the enquiry form: your name, phone number or e-mail address, and the content of your message.

Why we process it

We use the data only to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you become a client, deliver the agreed services and maintenance.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to reply and prepare a quote. Client data is kept for the periods required by law.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or pass it to third parties for marketing.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing and to object. Send requests to info@fidelis-automatika.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
