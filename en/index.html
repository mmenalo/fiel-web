Features

What your smart home can do

We build the system around your habits. These are the features our clients choose most often.

Central control

One wall tablet with a custom interface for the whole home, plus an app on your phone.

Smart alarm

One tap lowers the blinds, turns off the lights and locks the door. In a break-in, the siren and lights come on and you get a notification.

Smart lock

Automatic locking, shared access for family and guests, and a log of every entry and exit.

Presence simulation

While you're away, lights and blinds behave as if someone is home, deterring burglars.

Heating and cooling

Air conditioning and thermostats from anywhere. A comfortable temperature when you arrive, with lower energy use.

Lighting and blinds

Automated lighting scenes and blinds that follow the time of day, for comfort and better energy efficiency.

Smart scenes

Morning, night, movie or away: one tap and lighting, blinds and temperature adjust themselves.

Pool equipment

Filtration, heating, lighting and water level in one place, with water temperature and quality monitoring.

A network you can rely on

We run our systems on professional Ubiquiti UniFi network equipment: stable coverage across the whole home, 24/7 operation and business-grade protection for connected devices.

FAQ

Questions we're asked most often

Can a smart home be installed in an existing apartment or house?

Yes. The system is modular and in most cases needs only minor changes to the existing electrical installation, without major construction work. During the consultation we assess what is possible in your space.

How much does a smart home cost?

It depends on the size of the space and the features you want. That's why we start with a free consultation and then send a detailed quote with no hidden costs.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Core functions such as lighting, blinds, heating and the alarm run locally, without the internet. You only need the internet for remote control and phone notifications.

Can I expand the system later?

Yes. You can start with a basic package and add rooms or features later, such as video surveillance, a smart lock or pool control.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the scope of the project. You get an estimated timeline with the quote, and we plan the work to disrupt your daily life as little as possible.

Do you offer a warranty and maintenance?

All equipment and work come with a 2-year warranty. We also offer regular maintenance and technical support.
